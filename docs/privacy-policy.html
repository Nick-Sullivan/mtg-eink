Privacy Policy

mtg-eink — last updated September 2026

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. The app has no sign-up, login, or user profiles.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries of any kind.
  • No internet access. The app does not request network permission and never connects to the internet.
  • Photos. If you choose a photo to put on a display, it is picked through Android's system photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you select. Photos you save to the in-app library are copied to the app's private storage on your device.
  • Local data only. Your saved designs and tokens are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • NFC. The app uses NFC only to send images to an NFC-powered e-paper display you hold against your phone. Nothing is read from or sent to any other device.
